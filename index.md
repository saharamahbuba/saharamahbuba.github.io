## About Me

I am a Civil Engineering graduate from the **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)** with a strong interest in oceanography, particularly coastal and marine environmental systems.

My research interests focus on the transport and transformation of carbon, nutrients, and contaminants within aquatic environments, with emphasis on biogeochemical processes, water quality, wastewater management, and microplastic pollution.

I am trained in numerical methods and computational tools for analyzing complex systems, and I aim to pursue interdisciplinary environmental research involving coastal processes and aquatic environmental quality.
